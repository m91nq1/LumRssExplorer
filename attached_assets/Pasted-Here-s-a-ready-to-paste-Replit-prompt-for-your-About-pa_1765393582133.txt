### Replit Prompt – Build an “About LUM News” Page with Icons

In this existing **LUM News** project, create a dedicated **About** page/route (or section) that explains:

* What LUM News does
* Why someone should use it
* Who it’s for

Use the same visual language as the rest of the app (deep indigo / space theme).

---

### Requirements

1. **Page layout & theme**

* Create an `About` page (e.g. `/about` route or `about.html`), linked from the main nav “About”.
* Use the existing **deep indigo / space** background (`#1E174B` or similar), with subtle gradients/stars.
* Keep the layout responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Max-width centered content (e.g. 960–1200px) with good spacing and readable line length.

---

### 2. Hero section (“About LUM News”)

Top of the page:

* Title: **“About LUM News”**
* Short subtitle:

  > “An AI-powered global news lens that helps you see how the world is telling the same story.”
* Optional small tag above title: “News Intelligence · Multi-Region · AI-Summarised”.
* Place a subtle icon or illustration next to the title (e.g. a globe, radar, or eye icon).

---

### 3. Section: What LUM News does

Create a **two-column** section:

* Left: Text content.
* Right: Simple illustration or icon stack.

Text should explain in **plain language**:

* LUM News **aggregates news** from many sources and regions.
* It **clusters related articles into “stories”** so you see the event, not just random headlines.
* It uses **AI to summarise and compare coverage**, helping you understand sentiment and bias.
* You can **filter by topics and regions** (war, AI, Europe politics, etc.) and save stories you care about.

Use a small **icon grid** or bullets with icons (SVG or emoji placeholders), for example:

* 🛰️ **Global perspective** – See stories from US, Europe, China, Russia and more in one place.
* 🧠 **AI summaries** – Get fast, neutral summaries of complex stories.
* 📊 **Coverage & sentiment** – Understand who is covering what, and how they frame it.
* ⭐ **Saved stories** – Bookmark the narratives you want to follow.

---

### 4. Section: Why you should use it (benefits)

Add a section titled **“Why use LUM News?”** with **3–4 interactive cards**, each with an icon:

Each card should have:

* An icon at the top (use simple vector/emoji placeholders: 🔍, 🌍, ⚖️, ⏱️ etc.).
* A short heading.
* 2–3 sentence description.

Example cards:

1. **See every side of the story** (icon: 🌍)

   * Explain how different outlets/regions cover the same event differently.

2. **Cut through information overload** (icon: 🔍)

   * Explain how clustering + AI summaries save time.

3. **Track topics that matter to you** (icon: ⭐)

   * Explain topics/regions/alerts.

4. **Turn news into intelligence** (icon: 📊)

   * Explain that it’s built for people who need to understand patterns, not just read headlines.

Add hover effects on cards (slight lift/scale, glow border) to make them feel interactive.

---

### 5. Section: Who this is for

Add a section titled **“Who is LUM News for?”** with three columns/cards, each with an icon + persona:

1. **Analysts & Researchers** (icon: 📈)

   * Short blurb: geopolitical analysts, risk teams, policy researchers who need multi-source views.

2. **Journalists & Editors** (icon: 📰)

   * Short blurb: reporters who want to see emerging and under-reported stories across regions.

3. **Curious Power News Readers** (icon: 🧭)

   * Short blurb: informed individuals who want more than a single feed or echo chamber.

Each card should include:

* Icon
* Persona title
* 2–3 lines of description

---

### 6. Section: How it works (brief flow with icons)

Add a horizontal **3-step timeline** or vertical list titled **“How LUM News works”**:

Steps with icons:

1. **Collect** (icon: 🌐)

   * “We aggregate stories from multiple global sources and regions.”

2. **Cluster & Analyse** (icon: 🧠)

   * “AI groups related articles into stories, extracting entities, sentiment, and coverage patterns.”

3. **Present & Personalise** (icon: 🎯)

   * “You explore via topics, regions, and lenses, and save the narratives that matter to you.”

Use connecting lines/arrows or subtle animations on hover to make the timeline engaging.

---

### 7. Section: Trust & Transparency (optional but recommended)

Add a small section on **trust**:

* Heading: **“Built for clarity, not clickbait”**.
* Short paragraphs explaining:

  * You show multiple sources side-by-side.
  * AI summaries aim to be neutral and transparent.
  * Users can always click through to the **original articles**.

Use a shield/check icon (🛡️ or ✔️) for visual emphasis.

---

### 8. Call to action at the bottom

Finish with a centered CTA section:

* Heading: **“Ready to see the world’s narratives differently?”**
* Subtext: 1 sentence.
* Button: **“Explore LUM News”** – link back to the main landing/dashboard page.
* Secondary link: “Learn more about the product” if you have a Features page.

---

### Visual & interaction details

* Reuse your **indigo space palette** (hero blue, accent purple) and typography from the homepage.
* Icons can be:

  * Inline SVGs,
  * A simple icon font,
  * Or emoji placeholders styled nicely.
* On hover, cards and icon blocks should:

  * Slightly scale up,
  * Increase shadow/glow,
  * Have smooth transitions (`transition: all 0.2s–0.3s ease-out`).

When I navigate to the **About** page in this LUM News project, I see:

* A clean, visually consistent page that:

  * Explains **what LUM News does**,
  * Shows **why it’s useful**,
  * Clarifies **who it’s for**,
* With several **icon-based sections** and hover effects that make the page feel interactive and modern.
