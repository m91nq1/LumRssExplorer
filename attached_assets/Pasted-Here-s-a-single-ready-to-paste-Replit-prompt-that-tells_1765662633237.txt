#### 2. Privacy Policy content (for a news/intel app like LUM News)

Create sections with clear headings (using `<h2>`/`<h3>`):

1. **Introduction**

   * Explain what LUM News is (AI-assisted global news aggregation and analysis).
   * Mention that this policy explains how you collect, use, and protect information.

2. **Information We Collect**

   * **Account information:** email, password hash, display name.
   * **Usage data:** pages visited, filters used, clicks, bookmarks, approximate location (if used), device/browser info.
   * **Content preferences:** selected topics, regions, saved stories, alert settings.
   * **Communication data:** email address for digests/alerts, support messages.
   * Clarify that LUM News pulls **news articles from third-party sources** (APIs/RSS) but does not own that content.

3. **How We Use Your Information**

   * To provide and operate the service (show stories, personalize feeds).
   * To improve the product (analytics, feature development).
   * To send:

     * Account emails (verification, security).
     * Optional digests/alerts based on topics/regions.
   * To prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

4. **AI & Third-Party Services**

   * Explain that:

     * AI models may be used for clustering, summarization, and sentiment analysis.
     * Third-party APIs (e.g. news providers, email services such as Resend/Mailtrap, analytics, error logging) may process limited user data.
   * State that these providers are bound by their own privacy terms.
   * Clarify that AI summaries are generated from third-party news articles and may not be perfectly accurate.

5. **Cookies & Tracking**

   * Describe use of cookies or localStorage for:

     * Keeping users logged in.
     * Remembering preferences (topics, regions, theme).
     * Basic analytics.
   * Mention that users can control cookies via browser settings.

6. **Data Sharing & Disclosure**

   * State you do **not** sell personal data.
   * List cases where data may be shared:

     * Service providers (hosting, email, analytics, error monitoring).
     * Legal requirements or to protect rights/security.
   * Aggregated/anonymous stats may be shared publicly (e.g. total users, most viewed topics).

7. **Data Retention**

   * Explain how long you keep:

     * Account data (while account is active + reasonable period).
     * Logs/analytics (shorter, summarized).
     * Email preferences and saved stories.

8. **Security**

   * Briefly describe reasonable security measures (encryption in transit, access controls, etc.).
   * Clarify that no system is 100% secure.

9. **Your Rights & Choices**

   * Users can:

     * Access and update account info.
     * Change topic/region preferences.
     * Opt in/out of emails.
     * Request deletion of their account/data (with a simple explanation of the process).
   * These rights should be written generically (not jurisdiction-specific) with a note to contact support.

10. **International Transfers**

    * Mention that data may be stored/processed in other countries depending on hosting/third-party providers.

11. **Contact Us**

    * Provide a placeholder contact email for privacy questions (e.g. `privacy@lumnews.app`).

12. **Changes to This Policy**

    * Explain that the policy may change and the “Last updated” date will be adjusted.
    * Add a “Last updated: [placeholder date]” line at the top.
