# 10. Fair Use

Where United States law applies, LUM News may rely on the doctrine of fair use where appropriate.

Fair use is a fact-specific legal doctrine. Factors commonly considered include:

1. the purpose and character of the use;
2. the nature of the copyrighted work;
3. the amount and substantiality of the portion used; and
4. the effect of the use on the potential market for the copyrighted work.

No single factor automatically determines whether a particular use qualifies as fair use.

Accordingly, LUM News does not guarantee that every use of third-party material is protected by fair use.

Where appropriate, LUM News may consider:

* whether the use is informational, educational, analytical, critical, or transformative;
* whether only the amount reasonably necessary for the purpose is used;
* whether the source is identified;
* whether the use substitutes for the original work;
* and whether the use is otherwise authorized by the rights holder or applicable law.

# 17. DMCA Notices

For material to which the Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") applies, LUM News may process valid notices under Section 512 of the U.S. Copyright Act.

The DMCA provides a framework under which qualifying service providers may receive notifications of claimed copyright infringement.

A DMCA notice should contain substantially the information required by applicable law, including:

1. **Signature**
   A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

2. **Identification of the copyrighted work**
   Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed. If multiple works are involved, a representative list may be provided where permitted.

3. **Identification of the allegedly infringing material**
   Identification of the material or activity claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient to allow LUM News to locate it.

4. **Contact information**
   Information reasonably sufficient to permit LUM News to contact the complaining party, such as:

   * full name;
   * email address;
   * physical address; and/or
   * telephone number.

5. **Good-faith statement**
   A statement that the complaining party has a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, the owner's agent, or the law.

6. **Accuracy and authority statement**
   A statement that the information in the notification is accurate and, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

These elements reflect the requirements described by the U.S. Copyright Office for DMCA notifications.

# 24. Counter-Notification

If LUM News removes or disables material in response to a DMCA notice, the affected party may have the right to submit a DMCA counter-notification.

A counter-notification generally needs to identify:

1. the material that was removed or disabled;
2. where the material appeared before removal;
3. the individual's name, address, and telephone number;
4. a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the applicable U.S. federal district court, where required;
5. a statement accepting service of process from the complaining party;
6. a statement made under penalty of perjury that the person has a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled because of mistake or misidentification; and
7. a physical or electronic signature.

The exact requirements may depend on the applicable law and circumstances.
